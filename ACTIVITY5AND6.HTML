🎵 SMOKING BEHIND THE SUPERMARKET WITH YOU 🎵

Introduction

Smoking Behind the Supermarket with You is a slice-of-life romantic comedy manga about an unexpected friendship that develops between two people after work.

What I Like About the Anime

💙 Characters

The characters feel natural and interesting because their personalities are different but complement each other.

🎵 Relationship

I like how their relationship develops through simple conversations and everyday moments.

🌙 Story

The story is relaxing and focuses on friendship, work life, humor, and the small moments between people.

About the Story

🏪 The Supermarket

The supermarket is important because it is where the main characters' lives unexpectedly connect. Their meetings after work create a comfortable place for them to talk and relax.

🌙 After Work

Their time together after work becomes a simple but meaningful part of their daily routine.
